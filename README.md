\# Supermarket Sales Analysis



\## Project Overview



This project analyses supermarket sales data to identify sales trends, customer purchasing patterns, product performance, and opportunities for business improvement.



The goal is to turn raw sales data into meaningful insights that can support better business decisions.



\## Project Objectives



\* Analyse sales performance across branches and cities.

\* Identify the best-performing product lines.

\* Compare sales by customer type and gender.

\* Examine payment methods and purchasing patterns.

\* Explore monthly and hourly sales trends.

\* Analyse customer ratings and their relationship with sales.

\* Build an interactive Power BI dashboard.

\## Key Findings



\### Sales Performance



Total sales across the dataset were approximately 322,966.75, with total gross income of approximately 15,379.37.



\### Customer Gender



Female customers generated approximately 194,671.84 in sales, compared with 128,294.91 for male customers.



\### Customer Ratings



The correlation between sales and customer ratings was -0.04, indicating almost no linear relationship between the two variables.



\### Business Recommendation



The supermarket can investigate customer service, product quality, product availability, and checkout efficiency to identify opportunities to improve customer satisfaction.
